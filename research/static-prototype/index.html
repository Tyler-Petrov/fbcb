What we're about

A small Southern Baptist congregation where people know your name.

1907

The year our building went up on Main Street.It's still here, still brightly painted, and still where we meet every week. You can't miss it.

  • Jesus firstWe believe Jesus died for our sins and rose again, and that anyone can be saved by trusting him.
  • Reading the Bible togetherSunday mornings and Wednesday nights, we open it together and talk about how to live it.
  • Good neighborsWe pray for each other, show up for each other, and try to serve all of Bellflower.
