## Decisions from the Nov 8 meeting

**Website (Tyler builds it)**
- **Cost:** a domain at about $12/year, with free hosting.
- **Contents:**
  - service times, address, and the Sunday school and Wednesday Bible study times
  - a short church history and what the church does
  - the pastor and how to contact him, plus the church phone number
- **Events:** pulled from Facebook, so posting an event on Facebook updates the site.
- **Sermons:** link to the YouTube channel.
- **Cover photo:** the full-church photo from Christmas Eve. Tyler has it.
- **Also:** claim the church's free Google Business listing so searches show hours and services and link to the site and Facebook.

**Community Friendsgiving: Sunday, December 6, 2026**
- **Location:** the community building in Bellflower.
- **Schedule:** worship service there at 10:30 a.m., then the meal from 12:30 to 2:30 p.m. Guests are invited to both.
- **Who it's for:** open to the whole community. It's a free meal and a way to offer hospitality.
- **Food:** catered by the Bellflower Youth Club.
- **Menu:** turkey, mashed potatoes, gravy, green beans, dressing, rolls, salad, and pie and other desserts. Drinks are tea, coffee, and water.
- **RSVP:** by text to the church phone, with a deadline a few days before the food order. The exact date is still to be set.
- **Where the word goes out:**
  - posters at Hicks (grocery) and the community center, 11×14 or larger
  - a Facebook event, also posted to the Bellflower community page
  - door hangers for canvassing on Saturday, Nov 21 (10:00 to noon)
  - table tents at the meal with church info
- **Printing:** at the FedEx Office print shop from a flash drive, so the files must be print-ready PDFs.

**QR code**
- Points to the new website. Goes on the flyer, door hangers, and table tents.
