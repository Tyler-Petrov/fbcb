# Claim FBCB's free Google Business listing

It's free. Allow about 20 minutes, plus a few days for verification.

1. **Check whether a listing already exists.** Search Google Maps for "First Baptist Church Bellflower MO". If you find a pin, open it and tap **Claim this business** or **Own this business?**. If there's no pin, go to business.google.com and choose **Add business**.
2. **Sign in with a church Google account**, not a personal one, so the listing outlives any one volunteer. Add a second manager later, such as Pastor Jim or another member.
3. **Fill in the basics:**
   - Name: First Baptist Church of Bellflower
   - Category: Baptist church
   - Address: 302 S Main St, Bellflower, MO 63333
   - Phone: 573-340-1070
   - Website: the new site URL, once it's live
4. **Verify.** Google may offer a phone call or text to 573-340-1070, a short video showing the building sign and Main Street, or a postcard to the church address. Pick whichever method it offers that you can complete.
5. **Set hours** to match the services: Sunday 9:30 a.m.–noon and Wednesday 6:00–7:15 p.m. Use "special hours" for holidays and the Dec 6 Friendsgiving.
6. **Add photos:** the outside of the building, the Christmas Eve photo, and the sanctuary.
7. **Add links** to the Facebook group (https://www.facebook.com/groups/1522672238925411) and the YouTube channel (https://www.youtube.com/@FirstBaptistChurchBellflower) under the profile's social links.
8. **Leave out** the prayer list and any personal phone numbers.
9. **Afterward:** each time an event is posted to Facebook, you can optionally add a Google "update" for it too. Check the listing a couple of times a year for suggested edits from the public.
