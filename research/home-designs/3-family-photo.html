Beliefs
Read what we believe
The Bible
The Bible is God's inspired Word, true and trustworthy, and our final authority for what we believe and how we live.
God
There is one true God, who exists eternally as Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.
Jesus Christ
Jesus is God the Son. He was born of a virgin, lived without sin, died on the cross for our sins, and rose bodily from the dead.
Salvation
Salvation is a free gift of God's grace. It is received by turning from sin and trusting in Jesus, not earned by good works.
The church
A local church is a family of baptized believers who gather to worship, grow in the faith, serve, and share the good news.
Baptism and the Lord's Supper
Baptism by immersion pictures a believer's new life in Christ. The Lord's Supper remembers his death until he comes again.
His return
Jesus will return personally and visibly to judge the world and make all things new.

We affirm the Baptist Faith & Message (2000), the statement of faith shared by Southern Baptist churches.
